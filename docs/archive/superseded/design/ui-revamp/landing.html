How it works

A conversation that grows a research file.

No forms, no modes. You talk; the page beside you fills in, one honest section at a time.

1

Think out loud

Brainstorm freely. When an idea firms up, Thoth offers to keep it. It never turns a passing thought into a plan.

2

Argue with people who thought hardest

Ten thinkers, from Popper to Ostrom, read your hypothesis blind, then disagree where it matters.

3

Put it on trial

Papers become witnesses. A neutral court rules, and says so plainly when the evidence is insufficient.
