# Thoth: Instagram Reel plan

**Angle:** every AI chatbot hands you answers; Thoth starts from your hunch, lets great minds argue with it, checks
the literature, and **tells you when the papers don't support you.** The payoff is honesty, not hype.

**Format:** 1080×1920, 30fps, 18.0s (9 bars of dark phonk at 120 BPM; beat = 0.5s, bar = 2s). Loops.
**Tone:** cinematic, Obsidian & Gold (`frontend/styles/tokens.css`), with Newsreader / IBM Plex Sans / Plex Mono.
**Safe zone:** text stays in x 60–940, y 220–1540.
