# Operational Algorithm: Aristotelian Categorical & Logical Audit

```text
INPUT: Research Object R
OUTPUT: Structured ThinkerOutput & ReviewArtifact

STEP 1: ENTITY & CONSTRUCT EXTRACTION
  - Enumerate key entities, constructs, and categorical definitions in R.

STEP 2: DEFINITIONAL PRECISION AUDIT
  - For each construct C, determine:
      - Genus (superordinate class)
      - Differentia (what distinguishes it from sibling classes)
      - Essential vs. accidental properties
  - Flag vague or circular definitions as `CONCEPTUAL_AMBIGUITY`.

STEP 3: CATEGORY STRUCTURE & LEVEL CHECK
  - Inspect for category errors (e.g. treating an emergent process as a physical substance).
  - Detect level mixing (e.g. confusing micro-level agent states with macro-level market dynamics).

STEP 4: ARGUMENT RECONSTRUCTION
  - Convert substantive claims into explicit:
      Premises P1, P2, ... -> Inference -> Conclusion C
  - Test for:
      - Logical invalidity (affirming the consequent, denying the antecedent)
      - Equivocation (shifting meaning of terms across premises)
      - Hidden / unstated presuppositions
      - Circular reasoning (petitio principii)

STEP 5: EXPLANATORY COMPLETENESS (FOUR CAUSES AUDIT)
  - Evaluate whether the research supplies:
      - Material substrate / inputs
      - Formal structure / schema / architecture
      - Efficient cause / generative mechanism
      - Final goal / functional outcome / objective

STEP 6: CONTRADICTION & NECESSITY AUDIT
  - Compare stated assumptions against empirical findings and conclusions.
  - Identify whether premises are necessary for conclusions or merely coincidental.

STEP 7: CONTEXT-FIRST ASSIGNMENT & OUTPUT
  - If crucial theoretical definitions are missing from report context:
      verdict = INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT
      recommendation = WITHHELD_PENDING_CONTEXT
  - If material fallacies or category errors exist:
      verdict = MATERIAL_OBJECTION
  - If concepts are sharply demarcated, logically sound, and internally consistent:
      verdict = NO_MATERIAL_OBJECTION
```
