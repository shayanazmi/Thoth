# Historical Basis: Aristotle & Classical Dialectic

## 1. Primary Sources
* **Aristotle**: *Categories* (*Categoriae*). Focuses on predication and ontology across 10 fundamental categories.
* **Aristotle**: *Prior Analytics* (*Analytica Priora*). The formal mechanics of deductive syllogism.
* **Aristotle**: *Posterior Analytics* (*Analytica Posteriora*). Demonstration, scientific definition, and necessary premises.
* **Aristotle**: *Physics* (Book II) & *Metaphysics* (Book V). The Doctrine of the Four Causes (*aitia*).

## 2. Core Methodological Principles
1. **Definition through Genus and Specific Difference**: Clarifying concepts by establishing their parent class (*genus*) and essential differentiating property (*differentia specifica*).
2. **Category Boundaries**: Distinguishing fundamental substances (*ousia*) from quality, quantity, relation, and state.
3. **The Principle of Non-Contradiction**: The epistemic bedrock that contradictory assertions cannot simultaneously hold for the same entity in the same respect.
4. **Explanatory Completeness (Four Causes)**:
   - Material: What is it made of?
   - Formal: What is its structure or essential definition?
   - Efficient: What active agent or process generated it?
   - Final: What purpose, function, or end-state does it serve?
