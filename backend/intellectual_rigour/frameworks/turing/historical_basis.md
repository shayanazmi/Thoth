# Historical Basis: Alan Turing & Formal Operationalization

## 2. Core Methodological Principles
1. **The Principle of Effective Calculability (Turing Machines)**: Any process that can be mechanically calculated can be expressed as a discrete-state machine reading, writing, and transitioning across a finite alphabet and state space.
2. **Elimination of Implicit Human Intuition**: Replace vague words like "intuitively evaluates" or "heuristically reasons" with explicit state-transition functions $\delta(q, \sigma) \to (q', \sigma', d)$.
3. **Decidability and the Halting Problem**: Rigorously analyze whether an algorithmic procedure is guaranteed to terminate on all valid inputs, or whether it contains unhandled infinite loops or undecidable requirements.
4. **Operationalization of Questions**: Re-framing unfalsifiable philosophical debates into concrete, observable behavioral tests (e.g. the Imitation Game).
