# Historical Basis: Thomas Kuhn & Paradigm Analysis

## 1. Primary Sources
* **Kuhn, Thomas S. (1962/1970)**: *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions*. University of Chicago Press.
* **Kuhn, Thomas S. (1977)**: *The Essential Tension: Selected Studies in Scientific Tradition and Change*.

## 2. Core Methodological Principles
1. **Normal Science as Puzzle-Solving**: The overwhelming majority of scientific activity occurs within an established paradigm (*disciplinary matrix*), extending facts, matching facts with theory, and articulating theory without questioning foundational assumptions.
2. **Anomalies and Crisis**: Persistent mismatches between theory and observation that resist solution within the paradigm accumulate into a state of professional crisis.
3. **Revolution as Paradigm Shift**: Scientific revolutions occur when a competing conceptual framework displaces the old one, transforming standards of evidence, legitimate questions, and conceptual ontologies.
4. **Incommensurability**: Competing paradigms cannot always be compared by a neutral, universal metric because the meaning of key concepts shifts across frameworks.
