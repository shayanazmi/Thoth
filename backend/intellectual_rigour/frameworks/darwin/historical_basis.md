# Historical Basis: Charles Darwin & Generative Evolutionary Mechanisms

## 1. Primary Sources
* **Darwin, Charles (1859)**: *On the Origin of Species by Means of Natural Selection, or the Preservation of Favoured Races in the Struggle for Life*.
* **Darwin, Charles (1871)**: *The Descent of Man, and Selection in Relation to Sex*.

## 2. Core Methodological Principles
1. **Population Thinking vs. Essentialism**: Real populations exhibit continuous, intrinsic variance. Explanations must operate across distributions rather than ideal types.
2. **The Triad of Natural Selection**:
   - *Variation*: Differences exist across individuals/entities.
   - *Selection / Differential Reproduction*: Finite resources and environmental constraints favor specific variants over others.
   - *Inheritance / Propagation*: Successful traits persist and replicate across generations.
3. **Generative Mechanism vs. Mere Labeling**: A valid scientific explanation must specify the step-by-step physical or ecological mechanism capable of producing the observed structure from prior states without miraculous jumps (*saltations*).
4. **Scrutiny of Evolutionary Trade-offs & Vestigial Structures**: Natural processes produce sub-optimal historical compromises and path-dependencies rather than teleologically perfect engineering.
