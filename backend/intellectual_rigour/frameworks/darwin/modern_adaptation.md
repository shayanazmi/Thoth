# Modern Adaptation: Darwinian Mechanism Search in Complex Systems & AI

## 1. Domain Translation Matrix

| Historical Darwinian Concept | Modern Complex Systems & AI Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Heritable Variation** | Stochastic gradient descent exploration, genetic algorithm mutations, multi-agent strategy diversity. |
| **Environmental Selection Pressure** | Loss functions, RL reward penalties, computational resource limits, market competition. |
| **Generative Mechanism** | Explicit algorithmic dynamics that generate the macro-level macroscopic phenomenon from micro-level agent interactions. |
| **Spandrels & Path-Dependence** | Architectural artifacts preserved due to historical initialization rather than direct optimization; vestigial weights. |
| **Population Thinking** | Evaluating performance across distribution percentiles rather than relying exclusively on mean benchmark scores. |

## 2. Boundaries
* Darwinian evolution applies to systems with replication, variation, and differential selection; when applied outside biological domains, it is translated strictly as generative mechanism analysis and path-dependency tracking.
