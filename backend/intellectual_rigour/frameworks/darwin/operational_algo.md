# Operational Algorithm: Darwinian Generative Mechanism Audit

```text
INPUT: Observed Phenomenon P, Proposed Model / Theory M
OUTPUT: Structured ThinkerOutput & ReviewArtifact

STEP 1: VARIATION & DIVERSITY PROFILE
  - Identify what varies within the system (parameters, agent behaviors, phenotypic traits, data distributions).
  - Characterize whether the research assumes homogeneous uniformity or accounts for realistic variance.

STEP 2: DIFFERENTIAL OUTCOME ANALYSIS
  - Determine what persists, what fails, what propagates, and what gains advantage over time.

STEP 3: SELECTION PRESSURES & FILTER MAPPING
  - Identify the environmental bottlenecks, resource constraints, loss landscapes, and competitive filters acting on the system.

STEP 4: COMPETING GENERATIVE MECHANISMS
  - Formulate competing step-by-step mechanisms capable of generating observed pattern P:
      M1 (Proposed), M2 (Drift / Random Walk), M3 (Spandrel / Side-Effect), M4 (Alternative Selection)

STEP 5: CONDITION & SIGNATURE MAPPING
  - For each mechanism, map:
      - Necessary antecedent conditions
      - Observable intermediate signatures
      - Expected exceptions and edge cases

STEP 6: CUMULATIVE DEVELOPMENTAL PATHWAY AUDIT
  - Evaluate whether the proposed mechanism explains how the current state arose incrementally from prior states, or relies on an ungrounded sudden leap (saltation / teleological assumption).

STEP 7: CONTEXT-FIRST ASSIGNMENT & OUTPUT
  - If historical state transitions, population variance statistics, or fitness filter specs are missing:
      verdict = INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT
      recommendation = WITHHELD_PENDING_CONTEXT
  - If the proposed explanation is merely a descriptive label lacking a generative selection mechanism:
      verdict = MATERIAL_OBJECTION
  - If the generative mechanism is fully specified with explicit variation and selection constraints:
      verdict = NO_MATERIAL_OBJECTION
```
