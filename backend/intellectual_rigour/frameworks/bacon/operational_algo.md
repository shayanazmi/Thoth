# Operational Algorithm: Baconian Eliminative Induction Audit

```text
INPUT: Phenomenon P, Empirical Evidence E, Inductive Claims C
OUTPUT: Structured ThinkerOutput & ReviewArtifact

STEP 1: OBSERVATIONAL INVENTORY (THREE TABLES)
  - Map positive cases (P occurs), negative cases (P absent in similar conditions), and degree variations.
  - Detect whether evidence is selectively skewed toward confirmatory cases.

STEP 2: FOUR IDOLS AUDIT
  - Idols of the Tribe: Search for confirmation bias, overfitting, and pattern-seeking in noise.
  - Idols of the Cave: Identify narrow researcher assumptions, domain bias, and tool-induced blindness.
  - Idols of the Marketplace: Flag inflated terminology, buzzwords, and vague empirical metrics.
  - Idols of the Theatre: Detect uncritical allegiance to standard baseline benchmarks or fashionable theories.

STEP 3: EXCLUSION & ELIMINATION
  - For each competing explanatory factor F:
      - If P occurs without F -> Eliminate F as a necessary cause.
      - If F occurs without P -> Eliminate F as a sufficient cause.
  - Retain only explanations that survive systematic exclusion.

STEP 4: DISCRIMINATING TESTS (EXPERIMENTUM CRUCIS)
  - Formulate controlled comparative tests where competing hypotheses make conflicting predictions.
  - Prefer experiments capable of eliminating multiple alternatives simultaneously.

STEP 5: GRADUAL INDUCTION LADDER CHECK
  - Check whether the paper jumps directly from narrow observations to grand universal generalizations.
  - Demand intermediate axioms and incremental boundary validation.

STEP 6: CONTEXT-FIRST ASSIGNMENT & OUTPUT
  - If negative cases or control baseline ablations are missing from context:
      verdict = INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT
      recommendation = WITHHELD_PENDING_CONTEXT
  - If selective observation or unexcluded confounders undermine claims:
      verdict = MATERIAL_OBJECTION
  - If induction is systematically eliminated and controls are comprehensive:
      verdict = NO_MATERIAL_OBJECTION
```
