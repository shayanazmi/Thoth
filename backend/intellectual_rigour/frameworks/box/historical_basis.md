# Historical Basis: George Box & Iterative Model Criticism

## 2. Core Methodological Principles
1. **"All models are wrong, but some are useful"**: Mathematical and statistical models are deliberate simplifications of an infinitely complex reality. Rejecting a model merely because it is an approximation is unscientific; the true test is whether the model remains robust and informative for its specific purpose.
2. **The Iterative Learning Loop (Deduction $\leftrightarrow$ Induction)**:
   - *Deductive Stage*: Calculating consequences and predictions from the tentative model structure.
   - *Inductive / Critical Stage*: Analyzing residuals, diagnostic checks, and systematic discrepancies between model predictions and real-world data to drive informed model revision.
3. **Diagnostic Residual Analysis**: Unstructured, white-noise residuals indicate that the model has extracted the available signal; structured or autocorrelated residuals prove that the model is misspecified.
4. **Parsimony (*Ockham's Razor in Modeling*)**: Over-parameterized models fit noise; simple models that capture primary dynamics with explicit failure boundaries are scientifically superior.
